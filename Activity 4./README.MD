## 🎥 Exploring Google Meet

Google Meet is a video-conferencing platform developed by Google that allows people to communicate through virtual meetings.

It provides several features that make online communication and collaboration more convenient.

**Key Features:**
- Creating and joining online meetings
- Camera and microphone controls
- Screen sharing and presentations
- In-meeting chat
- Live captions
- Participant management
- Meeting links and invitations
- Virtual backgrounds and visual effects

**Practical Applications:**

Google Meet can be used for online classes, group meetings, academic discussions, presentations, interviews, and professional conferences.

It provides an accessible way for individuals to communicate and work together regardless of their physical location.

---

## 📝 Exploring Google Forms

Google Forms is an online application that allows users to create digital forms, surveys, questionnaires, and quizzes.

It helps simplify the process of gathering information and organizing responses.

**Key Features:**
- Creating customized forms
- Adding multiple question types
- Creating quizzes and assessments
- Setting correct answers and point values
- Collecting responses automatically
- Viewing response summaries and charts
- Connecting responses to Google Sheets
- Sharing forms through links

**Practical Applications:**

Google Forms can be used for surveys, student assessments, registration, attendance, feedback collection, and other information-gathering activities.

It makes collecting and reviewing responses easier and more organized.
