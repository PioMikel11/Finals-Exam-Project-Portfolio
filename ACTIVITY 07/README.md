# 📄 Activity 07 — Comparing AI-Generated Nursing Résumés

## Short description
I gave two AI models the same prompt and compared their résumé drafts on **structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality**. I used what I found to write a **one-page résumé for an entry-level staff nurse position** after graduation and licensure.

## How I compared the drafts

| Criteria | What I looked for |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Did the draft follow the sections I asked for, in the right order? |
| **Clarity** | Could a reader quickly find my clinical experience and understand what I did on duty? |
| **Formatting** | Was it clean, consistent, and short enough for one page? |
| **Overall quality** | Would I send it to a hospital with only light edits? |

## What have I learned?

* **Judge a résumé like a reader.** I scored each draft by how easily someone could find my clinical experience and understand what I did during duty.
* **One page forces choices.** My biggest challenge was fitting my rotations and leadership experience on a single page. I grouped my duties by rotation and removed skills I had already described elsewhere.
* **Specific examples make roles clear.** I wrote that I assigned duties as a student head nurse and coordinated ward activities as a student charge nurse. I also used the phrase **"under supervision"** when describing medication administration and IV insertion, so my scope of practice is stated honestly.
* **Tailor it every time.** For future applications, I can choose the most relevant clinical examples for each position and check every detail against my CV before sending.
