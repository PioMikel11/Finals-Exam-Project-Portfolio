# Activity 06 — My One-Page Nursing Portfolio: A Handoff Report 🩺

## Short description
I built a one-page website that works like a shift handoff report. As a fourth-year nursing student at Ateneo de Davao University, I wanted my classmates and clinical instructors to see, in one quick read, what my supervised clinical rotations taught me. The page also introduces our community research on health literacy and medication adherence among adults with type 2 diabetes.

## What have I learned?

* **Know your reader before you write.** I learned to write a clear prompt for a website builder by naming each section I wanted and saying who would read it. It is the same habit I use in a nursing handoff: say what the listener needs, in the order they need it.
* **Choosing is the hard part.** My main challenge was deciding which clinical experiences deserved a place on one page. I picked one example from each rotation instead of listing everything. My favorite is from OB triage, where listening to the patient's main concern first helped me focus my assessment.
* **My prompt became my checklist.** After the page was built, I went back to the section headings in my prompt and ticked them off one by one to make sure the site covered my introduction, my clinical experience, and my research.
* **A portfolio is never finished.** After each rotation, I can add a short entry with one skill I practiced and what it taught me, so the page grows as I do.

## What's on the page
* **Who I am:** a short introduction to me and my focus areas
* **Where I've trained:** one lesson from each clinical rotation
* **What I'm studying:** our community research on health literacy and medication adherence in type 2 diabetes
* **What's next:** how I plan to keep improving as a nurse
