### 🩺 Activity 05 — Enhancing AI Prompts for Nursing Education

**Short Description**

For this activity, I explored how improving the way I ask questions can help generate more useful and organized responses from ChatGPT. I focused on a nursing-related topic and compared the results of a simple prompt with a more detailed and specific one.

I started with a general question about preventing pressure injuries among hospitalized patients. After reviewing the initial response, I improved my prompt by requesting clear explanations, practical nursing interventions, and corresponding rationales. This helped me obtain information that was more relevant to nursing students and easier to understand.

**What Have I Learned?**

- I learned that the quality of an AI response depends partly on how clearly the question or instruction is written.
- A general prompt can provide basic information, but adding specific instructions helps produce more detailed and organized answers.
- I realized that asking for nursing interventions together with their rationales helps me understand not only what nurses should do but also why each action is important.
- Comparing different AI responses helped me recognize the importance of reviewing information instead of immediately accepting everything generated by AI.
- I also learned that ChatGPT can be a useful learning assistant when preparing nursing reviewers, understanding clinical concepts, and organizing study materials.
- Moving forward, I will apply better prompting techniques in my nursing studies while verifying AI-generated information using reliable references, current clinical guidelines, and hospital policies.

**Personal Takeaway**

This activity taught me that using AI effectively is not simply about asking questions but also about knowing how to communicate what information I need. As a nursing student, I can use this skill to make studying more efficient, improve my understanding of nursing concepts, and prepare for clinical responsibilities. However, AI should only serve as a learning aid and should never replace proper clinical judgment and evidence-based nursing practice.
